# Writing guide — aesthetic case reports and case series (English)

## Section intent
- **Title**: include "case report" / "case series"; lead with the phenomenon (complication, population, technique). ≤ 20 words.
- **Abstract**: case report — unstructured or Introduction / Case presentation / Conclusion per journal; series — structured (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions) with n, follow-up, key estimate + 95% CI.
- **Introduction** (2 short paragraphs): what is known (cite), the specific gap, why this case/series matters. End with the aim.
- **Case presentation / Methods**: follow the chronological order of the timeline; parameters in a table if > 4 values; photos referenced as Figures.
- **Results** (series): participants → intervention delivered → primary outcome → secondary → safety. Numbers only from `results.json`.
- **Discussion**: (1) principal finding in one sentence; (2) comparison with prior reports (from the novelty search — verified PMIDs only); (3) mechanism/causality reasoning, hedged; (4) limitations (single case / no control / unblinded / confounders / unstandardised photos); (5) practical take-away.
- **Conclusion**: 2–3 sentences; no claims beyond the design.
- **Patient perspective** (case report): short, approved by the patient.
- **Declarations**: consent, ethics, conflicts, funding, AI use, data availability.

## Style rules
- Past tense for what was done/observed; present tense for established knowledge.
- Hedge proportionately: "suggests", "may", "was associated with" — never "proves", "cures", "dramatic".
- Day offsets ("on Day 14"), not dates. Ages in years.
- Units: SI; mL, U, J/cm², nm, ms, mm; state the toxin product whenever units appear.
- No trade names in the title; generic first, trade name + manufacturer once in Methods.
- Figures: every figure has a legend with timepoint, view and what to look at. Photo panels labelled A, B, C.
- Word economy: target journal limits (check the current author guidelines — case reports are often ~1,000–2,000 words with ≤ 4–5 figures and ≤ 15–20 references, but this varies).

## Candidate journals (verify scope, article type, APC and AI policy on the journal site before recommending)
International: Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology; Dermatologic Surgery; Aesthetic Surgery Journal and ASJ Open Forum; Aesthetic Plastic Surgery; JAAD Case Reports; Clinical, Cosmetic and Investigational Dermatology; Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology.
Chinese: 中华医学美学美容杂志; 中国美容医学; 中国美容整形外科杂志.

## AI use
Authors are responsible for all content; AI tools cannot be authors (ICMJE / COPE positions). Most journals
require a disclosure in Methods or Acknowledgements — use `assets/templates/statements.md` and adapt to the
journal's wording. Do not upload identifiable patient data or photos to public AI services.
